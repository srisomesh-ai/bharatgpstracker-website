FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is installation included in the price?

Yes. Installation by our trained technician is included for orders within our service cities.

What happens after the first year?

You renew the tracking subscription at the yearly renewal price. The device remains yours.

Will the tracker drain my vehicle battery?

No. The device draws very little power and switches to a low-power mode when the vehicle is parked.

Can I track multiple vehicles with one account?

Yes. All your vehicles appear on a single map under one login, on both app and web.

Is engine cut-off safe?

Yes. Our technician will explain how the cut-off works and how to use it safely during installation.
